# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a thrilling sci-fi series created by the Duffer Brothers.  
It’s set in the 1980s and follows a group of kids as they uncover government secrets and face terrifying supernatural forces.

---

## 🧠 Main Characters
1. **Eleven** – A girl with telekinetic powers.  
2. **Mike Wheeler** – One of Eleven’s best friends.  
3. **Dustin Henderson** – Known for his humor and intelligence.  
4. **Jim Hopper** – The brave police chief of Hawkins.

---

### 🎬 My Favorite Moments
- When Eleven uses her powers to protect her friends  
- The epic showdown with the **Mind Flayer**  
- The nostalgic 80s setting and soundtrack  

> “Friends don’t lie.” — *Eleven*
